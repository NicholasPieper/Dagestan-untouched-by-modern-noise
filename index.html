Untouched by Modern Noise

The objective

Turn Chokh from a 40-minute photo stop into an overnight stay by selling time, not rooms.

Most visitors reach Chokh on a day tour from Makhachkala: they take photos, hike the nearby ghost town and drive on to Gunib by lunch. Etnodom's problem is that nobody thinks there's a reason to stay the night. So the campaign's job is to reposition a bed in Chokh as access to something priceless: travel back in time to a functioning Silk Road village.

The role each one plays
01 Stop the Scroll

Untouched by Modern Noise

Shareable cut · 15 sec · atmospheric, text overlay, no voiceover

Stop the scroll with dynamic cinematic cuts to show the place that looks like the past and plants the first question: is this real?

02 Make Them Want It

Waking Up on the Edge of the World

Cinematic single shot · 5–20 sec · POV, ambient sound only, unbroken take

One unbroken POV take from bed to balcony as the valley opens below. Lets the viewer imagine themselves in Chokh and sells the one thing a day tour cannot offer: the morning.

03 Book the Night

We Traveled 200 Years Without Leaving 2026

Unique experience · 30–45 sec · voiceover, host led, ambient sound bed

The host walks us through a day lived the old way, with the family who runs Etnodom. It reframes the room as access to a slower century and turns the viewer's interest into a booked night.
